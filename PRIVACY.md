# Privacy policy template

A template for apps that use **health_sync_kit**. Apple (App Store Review
Guideline 5.1.3) and Google (the Health Connect policy) both require a privacy
policy for apps that read health data. Replace everything in `[brackets]`,
delete what doesn't apply, and publish the result at a stable public URL.

This is a starting point, not legal advice. Check it against your own data
flows and the laws that apply to your users (for example GDPR, HIPAA, or
CCPA/CPRA).

---

[Company or developer name] ("we") makes [App name]. This policy explains what

## Health data we access

With your permission, [App name] reads the following from Apple Health (iOS)
or Health Connect (Android):

- [Steps, distance, active calories]
- [Heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability]
- [Sleep]
- [Workouts]
- [Weight, body fat percentage]

[If you write data:] [App name] also saves [the weight you log] to Apple
Health / Health Connect.

We only request the data types listed above. You can grant or deny each one
separately.

## How we use it

We use health data only to [show your activity, sleep and recovery trends in
the app]. We do **not**:

- use health data for advertising, marketing or data mining;
- sell health data, or share it with data brokers or advertisers;
- use it to make decisions about credit, insurance or employment.
